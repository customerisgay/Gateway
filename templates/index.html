Honest answers

Frequently asked questions

Does FamPay have an official merchant API?

No. FamPay is a consumer wallet and does not publish a public merchant collection API. Godpay verifies FamPay payments the way the ecosystem does — by reading the payment confirmation email your FamPay-linked Gmail receives, over IMAP, using a revocable 16-character Google App Password.

Does GPay have a merchant API?

No. Google Pay has no public merchant collection API for third parties. GPay is a payer app — it works through every rail here: UPI (any QR / deep link) and inside Razorpay / PhonePe / Paytm / Cashfree / MobiKwik checkouts.

Can I use official gateways instead?

Yes — bring your own merchant account and keys, paste them in Dashboard → Payments, and Godpay creates the payment, verifies the signed webhook, settles the order and fires your webhook. Same orders dashboard and REST API, no integration changes.

How does BharatPe verification work?

BharatPe's enterprise dashboard exposes a transaction feed merchants use to see QR collections. With your BharatPe Merchant ID and API token, Godpay polls that feed, matches the SUCCESS transaction to the pending order by its unique exact amount and UTR, and settles it.

Where does the money go?

Directly to the UPI VPA / bank account linked to your FamPay or BharatPe merchant account. Godpay never holds, pools or routes funds — we are verification infrastructure, not a payment aggregator.

What is the decimal offset, and can we turn it off?

To match payments without the customer typing a UTR, each order is quoted at a unique paise amount (e.g. ₹250 → ₹250.17). Toggle it off in Dashboard → Payments if you prefer exact amounts with manual UTR verification.

Is this legal for my business?

Godpay is a self-hosted tool for your own merchant accounts. You're responsible for complying with RBI/NPCI rules, FamPay & BharatPe terms of service, GST and KYC for your merchant account. Don't use it to process payments for third parties or unlawful goods.

What happens if verification breaks?

Orders stay PENDING until the payment window expires. You can use “Verify by UTR” on the checkout page as a manual fallback — your webhook never fires before verification succeeds. Confirm receipt before fulfilment.
