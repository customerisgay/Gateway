How GPay, PhonePe and Paytm work here

Direct UPI payments go to your configured recipient. Gateway payments use your provider account.

How credentials are protected

Gmail app passwords, BharatPe tokens and gateway API secrets are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) at rest, stored only in your self-hosted instance's database, and decrypted in memory solely to talk to the provider. They are never returned by any API endpoint or rendered back into pages. Rotate keys anytime in the provider's dashboard.
